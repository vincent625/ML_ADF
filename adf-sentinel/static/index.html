02 / DEFINE THE CASE

Actions are the unit of learning

Chronological release map

Reported release dates determine the map. Undated releases stay separate. Page-update and issue-closure times never replace release dates.

One ticket can contain different dated actions

What annotation must establish

  1. Independent action boundaries and release/variant scope.
  2. Decision cutoff and original text available at that time.
  3. Approved exact target, value, revision, and prerequisites.
  4. Implementing change, released bytes, and verification result.
  5. Reviewer identity, label strength, and disagreements.

A closed ticket and a “passed” comment are insufficient proof of an exact action. Proposed labels remain unreviewed.
