# Vision

Reduce the active engineering effort needed to understand, prepare, review, and verify an ADF change. Engineers retain ownership of scientific decisions, exact release scope, acceptance criteria, and approval.

The combined repository demonstrates requirements parsing, historical evidence analysis, review preparation, and limited local verification. The upstream goals of an installable candidate builder, automated scientific sanity testing, AI-assisted root-cause analysis, and substantial measured time reduction remain future work. Their original wording is retained under `docs/upstream/`.
