## Part-number handling for ADF #3 (v0.0.3)

For ADF #3 (v0.0.3), the DNA panel kit and DNA primer-tube part numbers use
temporary OCAPlus values until the Manticore panel is manufactured:

- DNA Panel Kit: `A40014056`
- DNA Primer Tube Pool 1: `100200225`
- DNA Primer Tube Pool 2: `100200226`

The RNA panel and RNA primer-tube values remain unchanged because Manticore
uses the OCAPlus RNA/Fusion panel.

The final manufactured-panel part numbers must replace these placeholders in a
future ADF release.
