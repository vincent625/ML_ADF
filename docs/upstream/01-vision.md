An ADF release can require approximately two weeks to build the bundle and another week for sanity testing. Engineers must translate every release-ticket requirement into bundle contents and tests, validate dependencies, troubleshoot failures, and resolve software bugs or conflicts. This largely manual three-week cycle delays assay availability and relies heavily on individual expertise. ADF Sentinel would combine AI reasoning with deterministic automation to ingest the release ticket and linked artifacts, generate a candidate bundle, convert requirements into traceable tests, execute sanity and regression checks, analyze failures, and assemble the release evidence. AI would recommend likely causes and corrections, while engineers retain control of defect resolution, scientific judgment, final validation, and release approval.
